randy.sh — black & white

Two palettes, same first screen, same content. Strict monochrome — hierarchy carried by weight, opacity, and rules rather than color. One warm accent reserved for errors and the boot pulse, so it means something when it shows.

Black — OLED-safe, near-black substrate
#0a0908 substrate — pure #000 is harsher than it needs to be and can smear on OLED sub-pixels; this is one notch up, imperceptible on OLED, kinder to LCD.

White — near-white paper, deep ink
