A FIELD GUIDE

Think beyond
the obvious.

  1. Seven prompts. You have 25 seconds to name one thing for each.
  2. A valid answer earns up to 100 points. Uncommon choices earn more. Each point takes Puff 10 metres deeper.
  3. One shared daily puzzle resets at midnight UTC. After that, keep exploring in unlimited mode.

Rarity is hand-ranked for this game, rather than measured from players. Answers come from a curated bank; spelling variants and small typos are accepted. If an answer isn’t listed, try another before time runs out. Your daily attempt and personal best stay in this browser.
