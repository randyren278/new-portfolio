FIRST Job Search privacy policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

FIRST Job Search is a personal, local tool used by Randy Ren to organize internship opportunities. It is not offered as a service to other users.

Google data

FIRST requests read-only access to the connected Gmail account. It reads LinkedIn job-alert emails from a rolling seven-day window so it can extract job titles, companies, locations, and application links.

Storage and sharing

Extracted job information is stored locally on the account owner's Mac. FIRST does not sell, share, or use Google user data for advertising. It does not modify, send, delete, label, archive, or mark Gmail messages.

Retention and access

The account owner controls the local database and may delete it at any time. Gmail access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security settings.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to randyren278@gmail.com.
