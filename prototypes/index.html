COUNCIL SUMMARY

Three councils converged strongly on the same skeleton: RENDER, PLATE, and TIDES are the load-bearing candidates because each answers Randy’s “why would this exist” test with a real-world referent (print production, cartographic navigation, fluid dynamics). CATALOG survives as the sharpest single-POV variation and the only one where the visitor has a real verb from second one. PRESS is killed — Fraunces on cream paper is now the signal of a portfolio Randy is explicitly not — and GLYPH is killed for scaling up the one good beat (the R-particle load-in) until repetition murders it. Two wildcards replace them: SOUNDING (TIDES stripped to its single strongest gesture — the tide-gauge cursor — which becomes the quietest direction in the set) and PRESSROOM (a hybrid of RENDER and CATALOG where the boot IS a printing press setting a terminal-syntax catalog). Palette is locked across all six: ink-black on warm cream, one considered ink-blue (Prussian / Payne’s grey-blue, ~#1F3A5F), no green anywhere. Two typefaces per direction, no Fraunces. Every motion must survive the question “what is this simulating?” — if the answer is “nothing, it’s just nice,” it gets cut. The boot ceremony, wherever it appears, is cookie-gated to run once per session and skippable by any keypress. Second-visit behavior is treated as a design surface, not an afterthought: mastery is the reward.
