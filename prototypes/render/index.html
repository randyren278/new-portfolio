PLATE 01 · ORYZO · CMYK · 300 DPI
↳ ESSAY

Voice orchestration layer for autonomous agents. Turned 300ms of latency into a feature — the pause the agent takes before answering became the moment you trust it.

We stopped hiding the wait. We staged it. A hairline appears; a caret ticks; an intent surfaces. By the time the model speaks, the user has already read it think.

The result: session length up 2.4×, escalation rate down by a third, and the first user quote we pinned to the wall — "it feels like it's actually listening." That was the whole product.

Shipped as a drop-in orchestration primitive. Two weekend integrations. Zero migration. A press mark, not a rewrite.

DISCIPLINEProduct · WebGL · Design
PLATE 02 · HALCYON · CMYK · 300 DPI
↳ ESSAY

Realtime collaborative canvas for design teams. Half a million multiplayer cursors and counting — most of them arriving at the same rectangle within a second of each other.

We rebuilt the presence layer from a scratchpad UDP transport up. Sixteen milliseconds between your cursor moving and every other cursor in the room reacting. On a laptop. Across the pacific.

The interface bet: no chrome, no menus, no overlays that steal the artboard. Every affordance draws itself only when the pointer earns it. Everything else is empty page.

Adopted by three studios you've heard of, one you haven't yet, and — briefly — the pixel-art team that ships the game you're probably playing right now.

§ PLATE 03

Aperture

↳
YEAR2024
ROLEconcept · prototype
CLIENTself-directed
DISCIPLINEMobile · System · Concept
PLATE 03 · APERTURE · CMYK · 300 DPI
↳ ESSAY

Camera-first mobile OS. The lens is the app launcher. Point at a receipt — expenses. Point at a book — library. Point at a whiteboard — the meeting begins.

The insight that ran the entire concept: apps are a filing metaphor from 1984. The camera is a pointing metaphor from 40,000 BC. We picked the older one.

Built as a research prototype on jailbroken hardware, then handed to two collaborators for user testing. The kids figured it out in eight seconds. The adults took nine.

Not shipped. Not going to be. Instead, three of the primitives live inside a device you probably own. Which is, honestly, more flattering.

§ PLATE 04

Fieldnote

MMXXIV
↳
YEAR2024
ROLEsolo · design + eng
CLIENTopen source
DISCIPLINECLI · AI · Tool
PLATE 04 · FIELDNOTE · CMYK · 300 DPI
↳ ESSAY

Terminal-native journaling agent. Writes with you, not for you. Prompts at first blank line, then gets out of the way. Never volunteers a paragraph you didn't ask for.

Every AI writing tool ships as a co-author. Fieldnote ships as a reader who occasionally underlines. Different job, different room, better book.

Currently used by roughly two thousand engineers as their pre-standup ritual. Sixty-four kilobytes. No cloud. Model runs against a locally hosted endpoint of your choosing.

Ships with three keybindings and one rule: if you don't type first, it doesn't type. That rule alone gets more mail than everything else combined.

§ PLATE 05

Signal Garden

MMXXIV
↳
YEAR2024
ROLEhardware + firmware
CLIENTinternal · team of nine
DISCIPLINEHardware · Interface
PLATE 05 · SIGNAL GARDEN · CMYK · 300 DPI
↳ ESSAY

Ambient e-ink dashboards for distributed teams. Status without Slack. Nine tiles, one wall, one glance — you know whether the fires are lit, and whether they should be.

The rule of the object: it doesn't ping. It doesn't buzz. If it changes, you can be told about it by someone who already noticed. That is a feature, not a bug.

Nine units in the field. Firmware over LoRa. Battery life measured in seasons, not hours. The one at the São Paulo desk shipped in June and hasn't been touched since.

Interfaces that live on your wall are not apps. They are more like weather. You learn them the way you learn the sky.

§ PLATE 06

Loom

MMXXIII
↳
YEAR2023
ROLEprotocol + interface
CLIENTexperiment
DISCIPLINEWeb3 · Audio · Experiment
PLATE 06 · LOOM · CMYK · 300 DPI
↳ ESSAY

Peer-to-peer music mesh. No servers, no ads, just rooms. You bring your headphones and a URL — the URL is the room, the room is the party, the party is until people leave.

Built on a summer as a small refusal. If the platform is the extractive object, then the protocol is the pastoral one. We drew the pastoral one.

Ran for eleven months in the wild before we quietly wound the seed nodes down. Roughly forty thousand sessions, most of them at 2am your local timezone.

Its epitaph, from a user: "the first internet thing i've used that didn't want anything from me." That's the sentence we ended the project on.
